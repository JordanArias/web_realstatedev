FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of properties do they offer?

We have a comprehensive portfolio that includes houses, apartments, land, offices, commercial spaces, and luxury properties, as well as pre-sale projects and investment opportunities.

Can I buy a property if I live abroad?

Yes. We serve Bolivian and foreign clients abroad. We coordinate virtual visits, send digital documentation, and facilitate the signing of contracts through powers of attorney or other legal mechanisms.

How do I know if a property has its papers in order?

Our legal team verifies that each property is free of debt, liens, and legal issues. We provide you with up-to-date documentation and explain everything in clear language.

How much does the brokerage service cost?

At Spencer, we work with transparent commissions based on the type of property and the agreement with the client. We always disclose costs upfront, with no hidden charges.

They offer properties for pre-sale or on plans

Yes. We work with trusted developers and offer pre-sale options with guaranteed delivery. These properties often come with preferential prices and easy payment options.

What documents do I need to buy a property?

For buyers, the most common requirements are a valid ID card and, if applicable, a marriage certificate. If applying for a loan, proof of income is also required. Foreign buyers can purchase properties by presenting their passport and meeting certain legal requirements, with our advice at all times.

They offer financial or banking advice

Yes. We can help you obtain mortgage loans through partner banks. We assess your profile and help you understand terms, rates, and conditions.

I can schedule a visit.

Yes, you can schedule in-person or virtual visits with any of our agents from the Agents or Properties section. You can also contact us directly using the contact form.

Can you help me sell my property?

Yes. We’ll help you throughout the entire sales process: from appraisal, promotion, and professional photography to negotiation and signing. Our goal is to sell your property at the best price and in the shortest possible time.

We offer personalized advice, legal support, access to a wide network of properties, specialized agents by area, and transparent support at every stage of the process. We aim to make buying or selling with us a safe and stress-free experience.
